1.  **Analyze Query:** The user wants to determine the optimal monthly production quantities for each of 141 aerospace-grade widgets (Widget1 through Widget141), considering labor, material, and byproduct constraints, in order to maximize total profit. Special attention is required for Widget3, which generates a valuable byproduct (CatalystX) that can be sold (with a sales cap) or must be disposed of at a cost if unsold.
2.  **Identify Model Type:** Based on the query, this is a Linear Programming (LP) problem with byproduct handling and resource constraints.
3.  **Define Index Sets:** The primary index is the set of widgets, \( i \in \{\text{Widget1}, \ldots, \text{Widget141}\} \). There is also a byproduct (CatalystX) associated only with Widget3.
4.  **Define Decision Variables:**
    -   `x[i]` = Number of units of widget \( i \) to produce in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative real numbers).
    -   `s` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX sold in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (bounded above by sales cap, non-negative).
    -   `d` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX disposed of in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
5.  **Identify Parameters (from Schema):**
    -   Objective coefficients:
        -   Widget profit per unit: from 'Profit' column in product_resources.csv.
        -   CatalystX sale price: $300/kg (given in query).
        -   CatalystX disposal cost: $200/kg (given in query).
    -   Constraint coefficients:
        -   Labor hours per widget: 'LaborHours' column in product_resources.csv.
        -   Material A per widget: 'MaterialA' column in product_resources.csv.
        -   Material B per widget: 'MaterialB' column in product_resources.csv.
        -   CatalystX generation rate: 5 kg per unit of Widget3 (given in query).
    -   Constraint RHS (limits):
        -   LaborHours: 5,000 (from resource_limits.csv).
        -   MaterialA: 24,000 (from resource_limits.csv).
        -   MaterialB: 15,000 (from resource_limits.csv).
        -   CatalystX sales cap: 1,500 kg/month (from query).
6.  **Formulate Objective:** Maximize total profit, which is the sum of (a) total profit from all widgets produced, (b) revenue from CatalystX sold, and (c) minus disposal costs for any unsold CatalystX:
    -   Maximize:  
        sum over i of [Profit[i] * x[i]]  
        + 300 * s  
        - 200 * d
7.  **Formulate Constraints:**
    -   Constraint 1 (Labor Hours):  
        sum over i of [LaborHours[i] * x[i]] ≤ 5,000
    -   Constraint 2 (Material A):  
        sum over i of [MaterialA[i] * x[i]] ≤ 24,000
    -   Constraint 3 (Material B):  
        sum over i of [MaterialB[i] * x[i]] ≤ 15,000
    -   Constraint 4 (CatalystX Mass Balance):  
        5 * x[Widget3] = s + d  
        (All CatalystX generated by Widget3 must be either sold or disposed of.)
    -   Constraint 5 (CatalystX Sales Cap):  
        s ≤ 1,500
    -   Constraint 6 (Non-negativity):  
        x[i] ≥ 0 for all i  
        s ≥ 0  
        d ≥ 0
