[Abstract Model Plan START]
1.  **Analyze Query:** The user wants to determine the optimal monthly production quantities for each of 141 specified widgets (Widget1 through Widget141), as well as the amount of CatalystX byproduct to sell, in order to maximize total profit. The model must account for resource constraints (labor hours, Material A, Material B), the generation and sale/disposal of CatalystX (a byproduct from Widget3), and the costs/revenues associated with CatalystX sales and disposal.
2.  **Identify Model Type:** Based on the query, this is a Linear Programming (LP) resource allocation and byproduct management problem.
3.  **Define Index Sets:** The primary indices are:
    - Widgets: \( i \in \{\text{Widget1}, \text{Widget2}, ..., \text{Widget141}\} \)
4.  **Define Decision Variables:**
    -   `x[i]` = Number of units of widget \( i \) to produce in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
    -   `s` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX sold in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
    -   `d` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX disposed of as hazardous waste in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
5.  **Identify Parameters (from Schema):**
    -   From product_resources.csv (all 141 rows required, filtered by Product column for Widget1–Widget141):
        -   `LaborHours[i]`: Labor hours required per unit of widget \( i \).
        -   `MaterialA[i]`: Material A required per unit of widget \( i \).
        -   `MaterialB[i]`: Material B required per unit of widget \( i \).
        -   `Profit[i]`: Base profit per unit of widget \( i \).
    -   From resource_limits.csv (all rows required):
        -   `MonthlyLimit` for each resource: LaborHours (5000), MaterialA (24000), MaterialB (15000).
    -   Byproduct parameters (from query description):
        -   Each unit of Widget3 produces 5 kg of CatalystX.
        -   CatalystX can be sold at $300/kg, up to 1500 kg/month.
        -   Unsold CatalystX must be disposed of at $200/kg.
6.  **Formulate Objective:** Maximize total profit, which is:
    -   Sum of (base profit per unit × units produced) for all widgets,
    -   Plus revenue from CatalystX sold ($300 × s),
    -   Minus disposal cost for unsold CatalystX ($200 × d).
    -   That is: Maximize \( \sum_{i=1}^{141} \text{Profit}[i] \cdot x[i] + 300 \cdot s - 200 \cdot d \).
7.  **Formulate Constraints:**
    -   Constraint 1 (Labor Hours): \( \sum_{i=1}^{141} \text{LaborHours}[i] \cdot x[i] \leq 5000 \)
    -   Constraint 2 (Material A): \( \sum_{i=1}^{141} \text{MaterialA}[i] \cdot x[i] \leq 24000 \)
    -   Constraint 3 (Material B): \( \sum_{i=1}^{141} \text{MaterialB}[i] \cdot x[i] \leq 15000 \)
    -   Constraint 4 (CatalystX Mass Balance): \( 5 \cdot x[\text{Widget3}] = s + d \) (all CatalystX generated by Widget3 must be either sold or disposed)
    -   Constraint 5 (CatalystX Sales Cap): \( s \leq 1500 \)
    -   Constraint 6 (Non-negativity): \( x[i] \geq 0 \) for all widgets, \( s \geq 0 \), \( d \geq 0 \)
