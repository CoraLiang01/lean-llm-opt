Decision variables:
- y_i ∈ {0,1} for i ∈ I: 1 if plant i is opened, 0 otherwise
- x_{ij} ≥ 0 for i ∈ I, j ∈ J: amount shipped from plant i to customer j

Mathematical Model:

Minimize total cost:
\[
\min \sum_{i=1}^{15} f_i y_i + \sum_{i=1}^{15} \sum_{j=1}^{15} c_{ij} x_{ij}
\]

Subject to:
1. Demand satisfaction for each customer:
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{15} x_{ij} = d_j \quad \forall j=1,\ldots,15
\]

2. Plant capacity (only if plant is open):
\[
\sum_{j=1}^{15} x_{ij} \leq cap_i \cdot y_i \quad \forall i=1,\ldots,15
\]

3. Non-negativity and integrality:
\[
x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i=1,\ldots,15;\ j=1,\ldots,15
\]
\[
y_i \in \{0,1\} \quad \forall i=1,\ldots,15
\]
