[Abstract Model Plan START]
1.  **Analyze Query:** The user wants to determine the optimal monthly production quantities for each of 141 specific widgets (Widget1 through Widget141), considering labor, Material A, and Material B constraints, while also accounting for the byproduct CatalystX generated by Widget3. CatalystX can be sold (up to a market cap) or must be disposed of at a cost if unsold. The goal is to maximize total profit, including widget profits, CatalystX sales, and disposal costs.
2.  **Identify Model Type:** Based on the query, this is a Linear Programming (LP) resource allocation and byproduct management problem.
3.  **Define Index Sets:** The primary indices are:
    - Products: \( i \in \{\text{Widget1}, \ldots, \text{Widget141}\} \)
4.  **Define Decision Variables:**
    - \( x[i] \) = Number of units of widget \( i \) to produce in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
    - \( s \) = Amount (kg) of CatalystX sold in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (bounded above by market cap, non-negative).
    - \( d \) = Amount (kg) of CatalystX disposed of in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
5.  **Identify Parameters (from Schema):**
    - From `product_resources.csv` (all 141 rows required, no filter):
        - 'LaborHours': Labor hours required per unit of each widget.
        - 'MaterialA': Material A required per unit of each widget.
        - 'MaterialB': Material B required per unit of each widget.
        - 'Profit': Base profit per unit of each widget.
    - From `resource_limits.csv` (all 3 rows required, no filter):
        - 'MonthlyLimit' for each resource: LaborHours (5000), MaterialA (24000), MaterialB (15000).
    - Byproduct parameters (from query):
        - Widget3 generates 5 kg CatalystX per unit produced.
        - CatalystX sale price: $300/kg; sales capped at 1500 kg/month.
        - Disposal cost: $200/kg for unsold CatalystX.
6.  **Formulate Objective:** Maximize total profit, which is:
    - Sum of (profit per unit × units produced) for all widgets,
    - Plus revenue from CatalystX sold (\( 300 \times s \)),
    - Minus disposal cost for unsold CatalystX (\( 200 \times d \)).
    - That is: Maximize \( \sum_{i} \text{Profit}[i] \times x[i] + 300 \times s - 200 \times d \).
7.  **Formulate Constraints:**
    - **Labor Hours Constraint:** \( \sum_{i} \text{LaborHours}[i] \times x[i] \leq 5000 \)
    - **Material A Constraint:** \( \sum_{i} \text{MaterialA}[i] \times x[i] \leq 24000 \)
    - **Material B Constraint:** \( \sum_{i} \text{MaterialB}[i] \times x[i] \leq 15000 \)
    - **CatalystX Mass Balance:** \( 5 \times x[\text{Widget3}] = s + d \) (all CatalystX generated must be either sold or disposed)
    - **CatalystX Sales Cap:** \( s \leq 1500 \)
    - **Non-negativity:** \( x[i] \geq 0 \) for all \( i \); \( s \geq 0 \); \( d \geq 0 \)
