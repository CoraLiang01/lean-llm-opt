Let $x_{ij}$ be the number of units of product $j$ to be placed on shelf $i$, where $x_{ij} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ for all shelves $i$ and products $j$.

Let:
- $S$ = set of shelves, indexed by $i$, with ShelfID as below.
- $P$ = set of products, indexed by $j$, with ProductName as below.
- $v_j$ = Value of product $j$.
- $w_j$ = Weight of product $j$.
- $C_i$ = Capacity of shelf $i$.

#### Mathematical Model

**Objective:**
\[
\max \sum_{i \in S} \sum_{j \in P} v_j \cdot x_{ij}
\]

**Subject to:**

- **Shelf Capacity Constraints:** For each shelf $i$,
\[
\sum_{j \in P} w_j \cdot x_{ij} \leq C_i \qquad \forall i \in S
\]

- **Integrality and Nonnegativity:**
\[
x_{ij} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0} \qquad \forall i \in S,\, j \in P
\]
