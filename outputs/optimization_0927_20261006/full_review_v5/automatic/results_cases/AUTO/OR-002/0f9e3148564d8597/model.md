##### Decision Variables

- $x_{ij} \geq 0$: quantity shipped from store $i$ to customer $j$ (continuous).

##### Objective Function

\[
\min \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
\]

##### Constraints

1. **Demand satisfaction** (each customer receives at least its demand):

   For all $j \in J$:
   \[
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j
   \]

2. **Supply capacity** (each store does not exceed its supply):

   For all $i \in I$:
   \[
   \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i
   \]

3. **Non-negativity**:

   For all $i \in I$, $j \in J$:
   \[
   x_{ij} \geq 0
   \]
