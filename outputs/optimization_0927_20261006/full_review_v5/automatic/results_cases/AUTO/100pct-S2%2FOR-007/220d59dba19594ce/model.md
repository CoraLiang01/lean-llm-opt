Let $v_i$ = Value (profit per unit of vehicle $i$)

Let $w_i$ = Weight (stock space required per unit of vehicle $i$)

From capacity.csv:

- Overall inventory capacity: $C = 765$

#### Decision Variables

For each $i \in I$:

- $x_i$ = number of vehicles of type $i$ to order per day

Domain: $x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ (nonnegative integers)

#### Mathematical Model

**Objective:**

$$
\max \sum_{i \in I} v_i x_i
$$

**Subject to:**

$$
\sum_{i \in I} w_i x_i \leq 765
$$

$$
x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0} \quad \forall i \in I
$$
