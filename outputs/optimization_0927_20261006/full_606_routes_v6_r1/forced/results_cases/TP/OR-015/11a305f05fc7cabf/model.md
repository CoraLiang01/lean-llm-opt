##### Decision Variables

Let $x_{\text{Aalopuri}} \geq 0$ denote the number of units of Aalopuri to fulfill.

##### Parameters

- Revenue per unit: $r_{\text{Aalopuri}} = 20$
- Demand: $d_{\text{Aalopuri}} = 1483$
- Initial Inventory: $I_{\text{Aalopuri}} = 10440.0$

##### Objective Function

$\max\ 20\, x_{\text{Aalopuri}}$

##### Constraints

1. Demand fulfillment: $x_{\text{Aalopuri}} \leq 1483$
2. Inventory limit: $x_{\text{Aalopuri}} \leq 10440.0$
3. Non-negativity: $x_{\text{Aalopuri}} \geq 0$

##### Variable Domain

$x_{\text{Aalopuri}}$ is continuous and nonnegative.
