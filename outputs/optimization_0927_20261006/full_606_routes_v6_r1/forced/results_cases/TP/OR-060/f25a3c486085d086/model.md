##### Decision Variables

- $y_i \in \{0,1\}$: 1 if supplier $i$ is open, 0 otherwise
- $x_{ij} \geq 0$: quantity supplied from supplier $i$ to customer $j$ (continuous)

##### Mathematical Model

Minimize total cost:
$$
\min \sum_{i \in I} f_i y_i + \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Subject to:

1. Demand satisfaction for each customer:
   $$
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} = d_j \qquad \forall j \in J
   $$

2. Supplier activation: a supplier can only supply if it is open:
   $$
   x_{ij} \leq d_j y_i \qquad \forall i \in I, \forall j \in J
   $$

3. Variable domains:
   $$
   y_i \in \{0,1\} \qquad \forall i \in I
   $$
   $$
   x_{ij} \geq 0 \qquad \forall i \in I, \forall j \in J
   $$
