##### Objective Function:

$\quad \min \sum_{i=1}^{15} \sum_{j=1}^{15} d_{ij} x_{ij}$

##### Constraints:

1. **Departure and Arrival Constraints:**

$\sum_{j=1,\, j \neq i}^{15} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i \in \{1,2,\ldots,15\}$

$\sum_{i=1,\, i \neq j}^{15} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall j \in \{1,2,\ldots,15\}$

2. **Subtour Elimination Constraints (MTZ Formulation):**

$u_1 = 1$

$2 \leq u_i \leq 15 \quad \forall i \in \{2,3,\ldots,15\}$

$u_i - u_j + 15\, x_{ij} \leq 14 \quad \forall i \neq j,\ i,j \in \{2,3,\ldots,15\}$

3. **Variable Domains:**

$x_{ij} \in \{0,1\} \quad \forall i,j \in \{1,2,\ldots,15\},\ i \neq j$

$u_i \in \mathbb{Z},\ 2 \leq u_i \leq 15 \quad \forall i \in \{2,3,\ldots,15\}$

##### Decision Variables:

- $x_{ij} = 1$ if the tour goes directly from location $i$ to location $j$, $0$ otherwise, for $i \neq j$.
- $u_i$ is the position of location $i$ in the tour (for $i=2,\ldots,15$), with $u_1 = 1$.

##### Notes:

- The tour starts and ends at location 1.
- The distance matrix is symmetric: $d_{ij} = d_{ji}$, and $d_{ii} = 0$ for all $i$.
- All locations must be visited exactly once, and subtours are eliminated by the MTZ constraints.
