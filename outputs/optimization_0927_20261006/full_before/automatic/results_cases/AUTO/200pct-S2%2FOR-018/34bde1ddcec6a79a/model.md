##### Constraints

   $$
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j

   - $x_{S1,C1} + x_{S2,C1} + \cdots + x_{S12,C1} \geq 52$

   - $x_{S1,C4} + x_{S2,C4} + \cdots + x_{S12,C4} \geq 103$
   - $x_{S1,C5} + x_{S2,C5} + \cdots + x_{S12,C5} \geq 32$

   - $x_{S1,C12} + x_{S2,C12} + \cdots + x_{S12,C12} \geq 31$

   \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i

3. **Non-negativity:** For all $i \in I$, $j \in J$,
   $$
   x_{ij} \geq 0
   $$

##### Complete Model

Minimize
\[
\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
\]
subject to
\[
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \quad \forall j \in J
\]
\[
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \quad \forall i \in I
\]
\[
x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
\]

with all coefficients and identifiers as listed above.
