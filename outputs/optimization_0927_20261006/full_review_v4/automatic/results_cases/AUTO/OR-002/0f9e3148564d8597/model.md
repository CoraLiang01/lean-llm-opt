##### Constraints

1. **Demand satisfaction** (each customer group receives at least its demand):

For each $j \in J$,
\[
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j
\]
That is,
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C1}} \geq 11 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C2}} \geq 1148 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C3}} \geq 54 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C4}} \geq 833 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C5}} \geq 154 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C6}} \geq 551 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C7}} \geq 7081 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C8}} \geq 76 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C9}} \geq 66 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C10}} \geq 174 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C11}} \geq 15 \\
&\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C12}} \geq 680 \\
\end{aligned}
\]

2. **Supply capacity** (each store cannot ship more than its capacity):

For each $i \in I$,
\[
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i
\]
That is,
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S1},j} \leq 4 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S2},j} \leq 575 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S3},j} \leq 1504 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S4},j} \leq 178 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S5},j} \leq 228 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S6},j} \leq 50 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S7},j} \leq 3 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S8},j} \leq 6148 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S9},j} \leq 6 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S10},j} \leq 10673 \\
&\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S11},j} \leq 174 \\
\end{aligned}
\]

3. **Non-negativity**:

\[
x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
\]

##### Complete Model

Minimize
\[
\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
\]
subject to
\[
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \quad \forall j \in J
\]
\[
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \quad \forall i \in I
\]
\[
x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
