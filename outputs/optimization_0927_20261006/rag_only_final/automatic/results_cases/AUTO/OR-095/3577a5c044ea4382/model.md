1.  **Analyze Query:** The user wants to determine the optimal monthly production quantities for each of 141 aerospace-grade widgets, considering resource constraints (labor hours, Material A, Material B), and to decide how much of the byproduct CatalystX (generated only by Widget3) should be sold or disposed, in order to maximize total profit (including widget profits, CatalystX sales, and disposal costs).
2.  **Identify Model Type:** Based on the query, this is a Linear Programming (LP) problem.
3.  **Define Index Sets:** The primary indices are Products (widgets, indexed by i ∈ {Widget1, ..., Widget141}).
4.  **Define Decision Variables:**
    -   `x[i]` = Number of units of widget i produced in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
    -   `s` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX sold in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (bounded between 0 and 1500).
    -   `d` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX disposed in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
5.  **Identify Parameters (from Schema):**
    -   Objective coefficients:
        -   Widget profit per unit: 'Profit' column from product_resources.csv.
        -   CatalystX sale price: $300/kg (external parameter).
        -   CatalystX disposal cost: $200/kg (external parameter).
    -   Constraint coefficients:
        -   Labor hours per unit: 'LaborHours' column from product_resources.csv.
        -   Material A per unit: 'MaterialA' column from product_resources.csv.
        -   Material B per unit: 'MaterialB' column from product_resources.csv.
        -   CatalystX generation rate: 5 kg per unit of Widget3 (external parameter).
    -   Constraint RHS (limits):
        -   Labor hours: 'MonthlyLimit' for 'LaborHours' from resource_limits.csv.
        -   Material A: 'MonthlyLimit' for 'MaterialA' from resource_limits.csv.
        -   Material B: 'MonthlyLimit' for 'MaterialB' from resource_limits.csv.
        -   CatalystX sales cap: 1500 kg (external parameter).
6.  **Formulate Objective:** Maximize total profit, defined as:  
    -   Sum over all widgets of (Profit per unit * units produced)  
    -   Plus (CatalystX sold * $300)  
    -   Minus (CatalystX disposed * $200)
7.  **Formulate Constraints:**
    -   Constraint 1 (Labor Hours): Total labor hours used by all widgets does not exceed the monthly limit:  
        sum over i of (LaborHours[i] * x[i]) ≤ resource_limits['LaborHours']
    -   Constraint 2 (Material A): Total Material A used by all widgets does not exceed the monthly limit:  
        sum over i of (MaterialA[i] * x[i]) ≤ resource_limits['MaterialA']
    -   Constraint 3 (Material B): Total Material B used by all widgets does not exceed the monthly limit:  
        sum over i of (MaterialB[i] * x[i]) ≤ resource_limits['MaterialB']
    -   Constraint 4 (CatalystX Mass Balance):  
        5 * x[Widget3] = s + d  
        (All CatalystX generated by Widget3 must be either sold or disposed)
    -   Constraint 5 (CatalystX Sales Cap):  
        s ≤ 1500  
        (Cannot sell more CatalystX than market allows)
    -   Constraint 6 (Non-negativity):  
        x[i] ≥ 0 for all i; s ≥ 0; d ≥ 0
