##### Objective Function

Minimize total transportation cost:
$$
\min \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

##### Constraints

1. **Demand satisfaction:** For each customer group $j \in J$,
   $$
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j
   $$
   That is,
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C1}} \geq 52$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C2}} \geq 80$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C3}} \geq 392$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C4}} \geq 103$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C5}} \geq 32$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C6}} \geq 1426$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C7}} \geq 1024$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C8}} \geq 2736$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C9}} \geq 1129$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C10}} \geq 676$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C11}} \geq 2631$
   - $\sum_{i \in I} x_{i,\text{C12}} \geq 31$

2. **Supply capacity:** For each distribution center $i \in I$,
   $$
   \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i
   $$
   That is,
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S1},j} \leq 58$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S2},j} \leq 32$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S3},j} \leq 6161$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S4},j} \leq 4$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S5},j} \leq 47$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S6},j} \leq 178$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S7},j} \leq 142$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S8},j} \leq 164$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S9},j} \leq 1011$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S10},j} \leq 6$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S11},j} \leq 7081$
   - $\sum_{j \in J} x_{\text{S12},j} \leq 948$

3. **Non-negativity:** For all $i \in I$, $j \in J$,
   $$
   x_{ij} \geq 0
   $$

##### Complete Model

Minimize
$$
\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$
subject to
$$
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \quad \forall j \in J
$$
$$
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \quad \forall i \in I
$$
$$
x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in I, j \in J
$$

where all sets, parameters, and coefficients are as listed above.
