##### Objective Function

\[
\min \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
\]

##### Constraints

1. **Demand satisfaction** (each customer group receives at least its demand):

   \[
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \qquad \forall j \in J
   \]

2. **Supply capacity** (each supplier does not exceed its capacity):

   \[
   \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \qquad \forall i \in I
   \]

3. **Non-negativity**:

   \[
   x_{ij} \geq 0 \qquad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
   \]

##### Complete Numerical Model

\[
\begin{align*}
\min\ & \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij} \\
\text{s.t.}\quad
& \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \qquad \forall j \in J \\
& \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \qquad \forall i \in I \\
& x_{ij} \geq 0 \qquad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
\end{align*}
\]

where all $c_{ij}$, $d_j$, and $s_i$ are as listed above.
