##### Decision Variables

For each $i \in I$, $j \in J$:

$x_{ij} \geq 0$ = quantity shipped from supplier $i$ to customer $j$ (continuous).

##### Objective Function

\[
\min \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
\]

##### Constraints

1. **Demand satisfaction** (for each customer $j \in J$):

\[
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \qquad \forall j \in J
\]

2. **Supply capacity** (for each supplier $i \in I$):

\[
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \qquad \forall i \in I
\]

3. **Non-negativity**:

\[
x_{ij} \geq 0 \qquad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
\]

##### Summary of Sets

\[
\begin{aligned}
I &= \{S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6, S7, S8, S9, S10, S11, S12\} \\
J &= \{C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6, C7, C8, C9, C10, C11, C12\}
\end{aligned}
\]

##### Complete Model

\[
\begin{aligned}
\min_{x_{ij} \geq 0} \quad & \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij} \\
\text{s.t.} \quad
& \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \qquad \forall j \in J \\
& \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \qquad \forall i \in I \\
& x_{ij} \geq 0 \qquad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J \\
\end{aligned}
\]
