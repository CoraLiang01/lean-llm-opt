##### Objective

Minimize total transportation cost:
$$
\min \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

##### Constraints

1. Demand satisfaction for each store $j$:
   $$
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \qquad \forall j \in J
   $$
   That is,
   - $x_{\text{S1},j} + x_{\text{S2},j} + \cdots + x_{\text{S10},j} \geq d_j$ for each $j$.

2. Supply capacity for each warehouse $i$:
   $$
   \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \qquad \forall i \in I
   $$
   That is,
   - $x_{i,\text{C1}} + x_{i,\text{C2}} + \cdots + x_{i,\text{C10}} \leq s_i$ for each $i$.

3. Non-negativity:
   $$
   x_{ij} \geq 0 \qquad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
   $$
