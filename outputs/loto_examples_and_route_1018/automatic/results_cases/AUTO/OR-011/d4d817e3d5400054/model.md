Let $x$ be the number of units of product id999 to fulfill.

Objective:
$$
\max\ 434.74\, x
$$

Subject to:
$$
0 \leq x \leq 8171 \\
0 \leq x \leq 56450 \\
x \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
$$

Where:
- $x$ = number of units of product id999 fulfilled (integer, $x \geq 0$)
- 434.74 = Revenue per unit
- 8171 = Demand for product id999
- 56450 = Initial Inventory for product id999

The fulfillment quantity $x$ cannot exceed either the demand or the available inventory.
