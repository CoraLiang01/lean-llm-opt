##### Constraints

1. Demand satisfaction (for each customer group $j$):
   $$
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \qquad \forall j \in J
   $$
   Explicitly:
   \begin{align*}
   x_{S1,C1} + x_{S2,C1} + \cdots + x_{S11,C1} &\geq 11 \\
   x_{S1,C2} + x_{S2,C2} + \cdots + x_{S11,C2} &\geq 1148 \\
   \vdots \\
   x_{S1,C12} + x_{S2,C12} + \cdots + x_{S11,C12} &\geq 680
   \end{align*}

2. Supply capacity (for each store $i$):
   $$
   \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \qquad \forall i \in I
   $$
   Explicitly:
   \begin{align*}
   x_{S1,C1} + x_{S1,C2} + \cdots + x_{S1,C12} &\leq 4 \\
   x_{S2,C1} + x_{S2,C2} + \cdots + x_{S2,C12} &\leq 575 \\
   \vdots \\
   x_{S11,C1} + x_{S11,C2} + \cdots + x_{S11,C12} &\leq 174
   \end{align*}

3. Non-negativity:
   $$
   x_{ij} \geq 0 \qquad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
