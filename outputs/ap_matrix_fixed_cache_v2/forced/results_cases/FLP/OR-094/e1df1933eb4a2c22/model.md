##### Sets and Indices

Let $M = \{1,2,\ldots,101\}$ index the radio models, with model $m$ corresponding to HiFi-$m$.

Let $W = \{1,2,3\}$ index the workstations.

##### Parameters

- $T_{w,m}$: Processing time (in minutes) required at workstation $w$ per unit of model $m$ (from workstation_times.csv, see below).
- $C_w$: Effective daily capacity (in minutes) of workstation $w$ after maintenance.

The effective capacities are:
- $C_1 = 1440 \times (1 - 0.10) = 1296$ minutes
- $C_2 = 1440 \times (1 - 0.14) = 1238.4$ minutes
- $C_3 = 1440 \times (1 - 0.12) = 1267.2$ minutes

##### Decision Variables

- $x_m \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$: Number of units of model $m$ to produce per day, for all $m \in M$.
- $I_w \geq 0$: Idle time (in minutes) at workstation $w$, for all $w \in W$.

##### Objective Function

Minimize total idle time across all workstations:
\[
\min \sum_{w=1}^3 I_w
\]

##### Constraints

1. Idle time definition for each workstation:
   \[
   I_w = C_w - \sum_{m=1}^{101} T_{w,m} x_m, \quad \forall w \in \{1,2,3\}
   \]
2. Idle time nonnegativity:
   \[
   I_w \geq 0, \quad \forall w \in \{1,2,3\}
   \]
3. Production nonnegativity and integrality:
   \[
   x_m \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}, \quad \forall m \in \{1,2,\ldots,101\}
   \]
