##### Decision Variables

$x_{ij}\geq 0$: quantity of cargo shipped from source $i\in I$ to destination $j\in J$ (continuous).  
$t_{ij}\in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$: number of trucks dispatched from source $i$ to destination $j$ (integer).

##### Objective Function

$\displaystyle \min \sum_{i\in I}\sum_{j\in J} c_{ij}\,x_{ij}$

##### Constraints

1. **Demand satisfaction**  
   $\displaystyle \sum_{i\in I}x_{ij}=d_j,\quad \forall j\in J$  

2. **Supply limits**  
   $\displaystyle \sum_{j\in J}x_{ij}\leq s_i,\quad \forall i\in I$  

3. **Truck capacity**  
   $\displaystyle x_{ij}\leq 10\,t_{ij},\quad \forall i\in I,\; \forall j\in J$  

4. **Non‑negativity and integrality**  
   $\displaystyle x_{ij}\geq 0,\quad t_{ij}\in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0},\quad \forall i\in I,\; \forall j\in J$
