##### Objective Function: 

$ \quad \quad \max \sum_i p_i \cdot x_i $

##### Constraints

###### 1. Capacity Constraint: 

$ \quad \quad \sum_i w_i \, x_i \leq C $

###### 2. Non‑negativity and Integrality Constraints: 

$ \quad \quad x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}, \quad \forall i $

Here $i$ indexes the real estate areas, $p_i$ is the benefit value for area $i$, $w_i$ is the weight (development effort) for area $i$, and $C$ is the overall development capacity.
