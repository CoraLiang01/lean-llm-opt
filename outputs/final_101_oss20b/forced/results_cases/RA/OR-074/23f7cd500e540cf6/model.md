##### Objective Function: 

$ \quad \quad \min \sum_{s=1}^{48} y_{s} $

##### Constraints

###### 1. Coverage Constraints for each 30‑minute slot $t$ ($t=1,\dots ,48$): 

$ \quad \quad \sum_{s=1}^{48} a_{s,t}\,y_{s} \;\ge\; r_{t}, \qquad \forall t=1,\dots ,48 $

###### 2. Non‑negativity and Integrality Constraints: 

$ \quad \quad y_{s}\in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, \qquad \forall s=1,\dots ,48 $
