### Model

Let  

* \(x_i\) = number of units of HiFi model \(i\) produced per day, \(i=1,\dots ,101\).  
* \(a_{ij}\) = processing time (minutes) of model \(i\) on workstation \(j\).  
* \(c_j\) = effective daily capacity of workstation \(j\).  

**Effective capacities**

\[
c_1 = 1440 \times (1-0.10) = 1296,\qquad
c_2 = 1440 \times (1-0.14) = 1238.4,\qquad
c_3 = 1440 \times (1-0.12) = 1267.2.
\]

**Objective**

\[
\max \;\sum_{i=1}^{101}\sum_{j=1}^{3} a_{ij}\,x_i
\]

(Equivalently, minimize total idle time \(\sum_j (c_j-\sum_i a_{ij}x_i)\).)

**Constraints**

For each workstation \(j=1,2,3\):

\[
\sum_{i=1}^{101} a_{ij}\,x_i \;\le\; c_j.
\]

**Variable domain**

\[
x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, \qquad i=1,\dots ,101.
\]
