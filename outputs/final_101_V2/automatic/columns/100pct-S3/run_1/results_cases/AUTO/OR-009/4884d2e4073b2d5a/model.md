##### Sets and Indices

Let $I$ be the set of areas (from ProductName in products.csv), indexed by $i$.

##### Parameters

For each area $i \in I$:
- $v_i$ = Value of developing real estate in area $i$ (from Value column)
- $w_i$ = Weight (resource requirement) for area $i$ (from Weight column)

Let $C$ = overall development capacity (from Capacity column in capacity.csv)

##### Decision Variables

For each area $i \in I$:
- $x_i \geq 0$ : scale of development per day in area $i$ (domain not specified as integer, so assume continuous unless otherwise stated)

##### Mathematical Model

Objective:
$$
\max \sum_{i \in I} v_i x_i
$$

Subject to:
$$
\sum_{i \in I} w_i x_i \leq C
$$

$$
x_i \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in I
$$
