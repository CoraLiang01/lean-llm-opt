##### Objective Function

Minimize total transportation cost:
$$
\min \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

##### Constraints

1. **Demand satisfaction:** For each customer group $j$,
   $$
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \qquad \forall j \in J
   $$
   (Each customer group must receive at least its demand.)

2. **Supply capacity:** For each distribution center $i$,
   $$
   \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \qquad \forall i \in I
   $$
   (No distribution center may ship more than its supply capacity.)

3. **Non-negativity:**
   $$
   x_{ij} \geq 0 \qquad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
   $$

Let $I = \{\text{S1}, \text{S2}, \ldots, \text{S12}\}$ and $J = \{\text{C1}, \text{C2}, \ldots, \text{C12}\}$, with parameters $d_j$, $s_i$, and $c_{ij}$ as above.

\min_{x_{ij} \geq 0} \quad & \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij} \\

& \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \qquad \forall i \in I \\
& x_{ij} \geq 0 \qquad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J

\]
