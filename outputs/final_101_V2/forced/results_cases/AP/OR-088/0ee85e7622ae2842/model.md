##### Sets

Let $I = \{F1, F2, \ldots, F15\}$ be the set of candidate plants.

Let $J = \{C1, C2, \ldots, C15\}$ be the set of customers.

##### Parameters

- $f_i$: Fixed opening cost for plant $i \in I$.
- $K_i$: Capacity of plant $i \in I$.
- $c_{ij}$: Per-unit transport cost from plant $i \in I$ to customer $j \in J$.
- $d_j$: Demand of customer $j \in J$.

From the retrieved data:

- $f_i$ and $K_i$ are given in cost.csv for each $i$.
- $c_{ij}$ is given in cost.csv for each $i, j$.
- $d_j$ is given in demand.csv for each $j$.

##### Decision Variables

- $y_i \in \{0,1\}$: $y_i = 1$ if plant $i$ is built, $0$ otherwise.
- $x_{ij} \geq 0$: Amount shipped from plant $i$ to customer $j$.

##### Objective Function

$$
\min \sum_{i \in I} f_i y_i + \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

##### Constraints

1. **Demand Satisfaction:**

   $$
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} = d_j \quad \forall j \in J
   $$

2. **Plant Capacity:**

   $$
   \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq K_i y_i \quad \forall i \in I
   $$

3. **Variable Domains:**

   $$
   y_i \in \{0,1\} \quad \forall i \in I
   $$

   $$
   x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in I, j \in J
   $$
