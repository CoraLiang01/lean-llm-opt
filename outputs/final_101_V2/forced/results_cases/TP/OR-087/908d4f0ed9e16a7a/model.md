##### Sets and Indices

Let $P$ be the set of products, indexed by $p$ (here, $P = \{\text{A1}, \text{A2}, \ldots, \text{A80}\}$).

##### Parameters (from CSVs, in source order)

For each $p \in P$:

- $D_p$: Maximum demand (100 kg units)
- $q_p$: Selling price (\$/100 kg)
- $c_p$: Production cost (\$/100 kg)
- $Q_p$: Production quota per day (100 kg units)
- $F_p$: Fixed activation cost (\$)
- $B_p$: Minimum batch size (100 kg units)

Number of production days: $T = 22$

##### Decision Variables

- $x_p \in \mathbb{Z}_+$: Number of 100 kg units of product $p$ produced in the month
- $y_p \in \{0,1\}$: 1 if product $p$ is produced (line activated), 0 otherwise

##### Model

$\max \left[ \sum_{p \in P} \left( q_p x_p - c_p x_p \right) - \sum_{p \in P} F_p y_p \right]$

subject to:

1. **Demand constraint:** $x_p \leq D_p \qquad \forall p \in P$
2. **Monthly production quota:** $x_p \leq T \cdot Q_p \qquad \forall p \in P$
3. **Minimum batch size (if produced):** $x_p \geq B_p y_p \qquad \forall p \in P$
4. **Non-negativity and integrality:** $x_p \in \mathbb{Z}_+, \quad y_p \in \{0,1\} \qquad \forall p \in P$
