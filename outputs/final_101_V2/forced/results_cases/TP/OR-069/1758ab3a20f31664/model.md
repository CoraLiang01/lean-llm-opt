##### Objective Function

Minimize the total assignment cost:
$$
\min \sum_{i=1}^{11} \sum_{j=1}^{11} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

##### Constraints

1. Each manager is assigned to exactly one project:
$$
\sum_{j=1}^{11} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i = 1,\ldots,11
$$

2. Each project is assigned to exactly one manager:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{11} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall j = 1,\ldots,11
$$

3. Binary assignment variables:
$$
x_{ij} \in \{0,1\} \quad \forall i = 1,\ldots,11;\ j = 1,\ldots,11
$$
