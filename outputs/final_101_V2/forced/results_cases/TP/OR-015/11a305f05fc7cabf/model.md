Let $x_{\text{Aalopuri}}$ be the number of units of Aalopuri to fulfill (continuous, $x_{\text{Aalopuri}} \geq 0$).

Objective:
$$
\max\ 20\, x_{\text{Aalopuri}}
$$

Subject to:
$$
x_{\text{Aalopuri}} \leq 1483 \qquad \text{(demand constraint)}
$$
$$
x_{\text{Aalopuri}} \leq 10440.0 \qquad \text{(initial inventory constraint)}
$$
$$
x_{\text{Aalopuri}} \geq 0
$$

Where:
- $x_{\text{Aalopuri}}$: units of Aalopuri fulfilled
- Revenue per unit: $20$
- Demand: $1483$
- Initial Inventory: $10440.0$
