1. **Device capacity constraints:** For each device $d \in D$,

   \[
   x_p \geq 0
   \]

##### Complete Mathematical Model

\[
\begin{align*}
\max \quad & \sum_{p \in P} u_p x_p \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{p \in P} t_{dp} x_p \leq C_d, \quad \forall d \in D \\
& x_p \geq 0, \quad \forall p \in P
\end{align*}
\]
