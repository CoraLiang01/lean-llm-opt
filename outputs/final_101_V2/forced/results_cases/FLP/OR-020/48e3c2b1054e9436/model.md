##### Objective Function

\[
\max \sum_{i \in P} r_i x_i
\]

##### Constraints

1. Inventory constraint: $x_i \leq s_i,\quad \forall i \in P$
2. Demand constraint: $x_i \leq d_i,\quad \forall i \in P$
3. Nonnegativity and integrality: $x_i \geq 0$ and integer, $\forall i \in P$

##### Complete Model

\[
\begin{align*}
\max_{x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_+} \quad & \sum_{i \in P} r_i x_i \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & x_i \leq s_i, \quad \forall i \in P \\
                  & x_i \leq d_i, \quad \forall i \in P \\
                  & x_i \geq 0, \quad x_i \in \mathbb{Z}, \quad \forall i \in P
\end{align*}
\]

where all $r_i$, $s_i$, $d_i$ are as listed above for each product $i \in P$.
