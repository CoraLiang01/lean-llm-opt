##### Decision Variables

- $x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_+$: quantity of product $i$ produced in the month (in 100 kg units)
- $y_i \in \{0,1\}$: 1 if product $i$'s production line is activated, 0 otherwise

##### Objective Function

\[
\max \left\{ \sum_{i \in I} \left[ p_i x_i - c_i x_i - F_i y_i \right] \right\}
\]

##### Constraints

1. **Demand constraint** (do not exceed demand):

   \[
   x_i \leq D_i, \quad \forall i \in I
   \]

2. **Production quota constraint** (do not exceed total monthly capacity):

   \[
   x_i \leq q_i \cdot T, \quad \forall i \in I
   \]

3. **Minimum batch size constraint** (if produced, must meet minimum batch):

   \[
   x_i \geq m_i y_i, \quad \forall i \in I
   \]

4. **Activation constraint** (cannot produce unless activated):

   \[
   x_i \leq (D_i) y_i, \quad \forall i \in I
   \]
   (This is redundant with the above, but ensures $x_i = 0$ if $y_i = 0$.)

5. **Integrality**:

   \[
   x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_+, \quad y_i \in \{0,1\}, \quad \forall i \in I
   \]

##### Complete Model

\[
\begin{align*}
\max \quad & \sum_{i \in I} \left[ (p_i - c_i) x_i - F_i y_i \right] \\
\text{s.t.} \quad
& x_i \leq D_i, \quad \forall i \in I \\
& x_i \leq q_i \cdot 22, \quad \forall i \in I \\
& x_i \geq m_i y_i, \quad \forall i \in I \\
& x_i \leq D_i y_i, \quad \forall i \in I \\
& x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_+, \quad y_i \in \{0,1\}, \quad \forall i \in I
\end{align*}
\]

where all parameter values are as listed above for $i = $ A1, ..., A80.

**All variables and parameters are integer and as specified in the data.**
