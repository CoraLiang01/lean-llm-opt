##### Decision Variables

$x_{ij} \geq 0$ : quantity shipped from supplier $i$ to customer $j$, for all $i \in I$, $j \in J$ (continuous).

##### Objective

Minimize total transportation cost:
$$
\min \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

That is,
\[
\min \Bigg(
\sum_{j=1}^{10} c_{S1,j} x_{S1,j}
+ \sum_{j=1}^{10} c_{S2,j} x_{S2,j}
+ \cdots
+ \sum_{j=1}^{10} c_{S10,j} x_{S10,j}
\Bigg)
\]
with $c_{ij}$ as given above.

##### Constraints

1. **Demand satisfaction** (each customer receives at least its demand):
   $$
   \sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \quad \forall j \in J
   $$
   Explicitly:
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C1} \geq 216$
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C2} \geq 168$
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C3} \geq 264$
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C4} \geq 216$
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C5} \geq 216$
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C6} \geq 192$
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C7} \geq 144$
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C8} \geq 168$
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C9} \geq 168$
   - $\sum_{i} x_{i,C10} \geq 168$

2. **Supply capacity** (each supplier does not exceed its capacity):
   $$
   \sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \quad \forall i \in I
   $$
   Explicitly:
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S1,j} \leq 288$
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S2,j} \leq 288$
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S3,j} \leq 264$
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S4,j} \leq 264$
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S5,j} \leq 216$
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S6,j} \leq 216$
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S7,j} \leq 168$
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S8,j} \leq 216$
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S9,j} \leq 240$
   - $\sum_{j} x_{S10,j} \leq 168$

3. **Non-negativity**:
   $$
   x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
   $$

##### Complete Model

Minimize
\[
\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} c_{ij} x_{ij}
\]
subject to
\[
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \geq d_j \quad \forall j \in J
\]
\[
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \leq s_i \quad \forall i \in I
\]
\[
x_{ij} \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in I,\, j \in J
\]
with all $c_{ij}$, $d_j$, and $s_i$ as given above.
