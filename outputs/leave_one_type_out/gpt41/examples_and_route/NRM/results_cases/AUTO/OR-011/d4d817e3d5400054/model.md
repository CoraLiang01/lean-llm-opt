Let $x$ be the number of units of product id999 to fulfill.

Objective:
$$
\max\ 434.74\, x
$$

Subject to:
$$
0 \leq x \leq 56450 \\
0 \leq x \leq 8171 \\
x \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}
$$

Where:
- $434.74$ is the revenue per unit of product id999,
- $56450$ is the initial inventory of product id999,
- $8171$ is the demand for product id999,
- $x$ is the number of units of product id999 fulfilled (must be a nonnegative integer).
