Let $x_i$ be the number of units of Baby Food_255.28 to fulfill.

Objective:
$$
\max\ 255.28\, x_i
$$

Subject to:
$$
x_i \leq 3066513 \quad \text{(Demand constraint)}
$$
$$
x_i \leq 22749210 \quad \text{(Initial Inventory constraint)}
$$
$$
x_i \geq 0,\quad x_i \in \mathbb{Z}
$$

Where:
- $x_i$ = units of Baby Food_255.28 fulfilled
- 255.28 = revenue per unit
- 3066513 = demand for Baby Food_255.28
- 22749210 = initial inventory for Baby Food_255.28
