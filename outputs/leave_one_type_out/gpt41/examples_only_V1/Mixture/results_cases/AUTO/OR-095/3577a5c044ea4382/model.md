1.  **Analyze Query:** The user wants to determine the optimal monthly production quantities for each of 141 aerospace-grade widgets (Widget1–Widget141), considering resource constraints (labor hours, Material A, Material B), and the special byproduct (CatalystX) generated by Widget3, which can be sold (with a sales cap) or must be disposed of (at a cost), in order to maximize total profit.
2.  **Identify Model Type:** Based on the query, this is a Linear Programming (LP) problem with byproduct handling and resource constraints.
3.  **Define Index Sets:** The primary index is the set of products:  
    - Products: i ∈ {Widget1, Widget2, ..., Widget141}
4.  **Define Decision Variables:**
    -   `x[i]` = Number of units of widget i produced in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (≥ 0).
    -   `s` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX sold in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (0 ≤ s ≤ 1500).
    -   `d` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX disposed of in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (≥ 0).
5.  **Identify Parameters (from Schema):**
    -   Objective coefficients:
        - Base profit per unit: from 'Profit' column in product_resources.csv (per widget).
        - CatalystX sale price: $300/kg (fixed, from query).
        - CatalystX disposal cost: $200/kg (fixed, from query).
    -   Constraint coefficients:
        - Labor hours per unit: 'LaborHours' column in product_resources.csv.
        - Material A per unit: 'MaterialA' column in product_resources.csv.
        - Material B per unit: 'MaterialB' column in product_resources.csv.
        - CatalystX byproduct: 5 kg per unit of Widget3 only (from query).
    -   Constraint RHS (limits):
        - Labor hours: 5,000 (from resource_limits.csv).
        - Material A: 24,000 (from resource_limits.csv).
        - Material B: 15,000 (from resource_limits.csv).
        - CatalystX sales cap: 1,500 kg (from query).
6.  **Formulate Objective:**  
    Maximize total profit, which is:
    - Sum of (base profit per unit × units produced) for all widgets,
    - Plus revenue from CatalystX sold (at $300/kg),
    - Minus disposal cost for unsold CatalystX (at $200/kg).
    That is:  
    Maximize  
    sum over i of [Profit[i] × x[i]]  
    + 300 × s  
    – 200 × d
7.  **Formulate Constraints:**
    -   Constraint 1 (Labor Hours):  
        sum over i of [LaborHours[i] × x[i]] ≤ 5,000
    -   Constraint 2 (Material A):  
        sum over i of [MaterialA[i] × x[i]] ≤ 24,000
    -   Constraint 3 (Material B):  
        sum over i of [MaterialB[i] × x[i]] ≤ 15,000
    -   Constraint 4 (CatalystX balance):  
        5 × x[Widget3] = s + d  
        (All CatalystX generated by Widget3 must be either sold or disposed of.)
    -   Constraint 5 (CatalystX sales cap):  
        0 ≤ s ≤ 1,500
    -   Constraint 6 (Non-negativity):  
        x[i] ≥ 0 for all i  
        d ≥ 0
