##### Decision Variables

\[
\begin{aligned}
y_i &\in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0} &&\text{(units of product }i\text{ produced, 100 kg)}\\
z_i &\in \{0,1\} &&\text{(1 if product }i\text{ is activated, 0 otherwise)}
\end{aligned}
\qquad \forall i\in I
\]

##### Objective Function

\[
\max \;\sum_{i\in I}\Bigl[(P_i-C_i)\,y_i - A_i\,z_i\Bigr]
\]

##### Constraints

1. **Demand and quota limits (activated products only)**  
   \[
   y_i \;\le\; D_i\,z_i \qquad \forall i\in I
   \]
   \[
   y_i \;\le\; Q_i\cdot 22\,z_i \qquad \forall i\in I
   \]

2. **Minimum batch size**  
   \[
   y_i \;\ge\; B_i\,z_i \qquad \forall i\in I
   \]

3. **Variable domains**  
   \[
   y_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, \qquad z_i \in \{0,1\} \qquad \forall i\in I
   \]

These equations fully describe the integer programming model that maximizes total profit while respecting demand, production quotas, activation costs, and minimum batch size requirements for all 80 products.
