**Mathematical Model**

Let  

* \(I\) – set of tablet models (identified by the *Product Name*).  
* \(r_i\) – revenue per unit of model \(i\).  
* \(d_i\) – demand for model \(i\).  
* \(inv_i\) – initial inventory of model \(i\).  
* \(x_i\) – number of units of model \(i\) to be sold (decision variable).

\[
\begin{aligned}
\max\;& \sum_{i\in I} r_i\,x_i \\[4pt]
\text{s.t. } & 0 \le x_i \le d_i && \forall i\in I \\[2pt]
             & 0 \le x_i \le inv_i && \forall i\in I \\[2pt]
             & x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0} && \forall i\in I
\end{aligned}
\]
