1.  **Analyze Query:** The user wants to determine the optimal monthly production quantities for each of 141 specific widgets (Widget1 through Widget141), considering labor, Material A, and Material B limits, and to decide how much of the byproduct CatalystX (generated only by Widget3) should be sold (subject to a sales cap) or disposed of (with a disposal cost), in order to maximize total profit.
2.  **Identify Model Type:** Based on the query, this is a Linear Programming (LP) resource allocation and byproduct management problem.
3.  **Define Index Sets:** The primary indices are:
    -   Widgets: \( i \in \{\text{Widget1}, \ldots, \text{Widget141}\} \)
4.  **Define Decision Variables:**
    -   `x[i]` = Number of units of widget \( i \) to produce in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
    -   `s` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX sold in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
    -   `d` = Amount (kg) of CatalystX disposed of in the month. Type: GRB.CONTINUOUS (non-negative).
5.  **Identify Parameters (from Schema):**
    -   From `product_resources.csv` (all 141 rows required, no filter):
        -   For each widget \( i \):
            -   `LaborHours[i]`: Labor hours required per unit.
            -   `MaterialA[i]`: Material A required per unit (kg).
            -   `MaterialB[i]`: Material B required per unit (kg).
            -   `Profit[i]`: Base profit per unit sold.
    -   From `resource_limits.csv` (all 3 rows required, no filter):
        -   `LaborHours` monthly limit: 5,000 hours.
        -   `MaterialA` monthly limit: 24,000 kg.
        -   `MaterialB` monthly limit: 15,000 kg.
    -   Byproduct parameters (from query, not CSV):
        -   Each unit of Widget3 produces 5 kg of CatalystX.
        -   CatalystX sales price: $300/kg, sales capped at 1,500 kg/month.
        -   Disposal cost for unsold CatalystX: $200/kg.
6.  **Formulate Objective:** Maximize total profit, which is:
    -   The sum of base profits from all widgets produced and sold (sum over all \( i \) of `Profit[i] * x[i]`),
    -   Plus revenue from CatalystX sold (`300 * s`),
    -   Minus disposal costs for unsold CatalystX (`200 * d`).
    -   That is: Maximize \( \sum_{i=1}^{141} \text{Profit}[i] \cdot x[i] + 300 \cdot s - 200 \cdot d \).
7.  **Formulate Constraints:**
    -   Constraint 1 (Labor Hours): Total labor hours used by all widgets cannot exceed 5,000 hours:
        -   \( \sum_{i=1}^{141} \text{LaborHours}[i] \cdot x[i] \leq 5,000 \)
    -   Constraint 2 (Material A): Total Material A used by all widgets cannot exceed 24,000 kg:
        -   \( \sum_{i=1}^{141} \text{MaterialA}[i] \cdot x[i] \leq 24,000 \)
    -   Constraint 3 (Material B): Total Material B used by all widgets cannot exceed 15,000 kg:
        -   \( \sum_{i=1}^{141} \text{MaterialB}[i] \cdot x[i] \leq 15,000 \)
    -   Constraint 4 (CatalystX Mass Balance): All CatalystX generated by Widget3 must be either sold or disposed:
        -   \( 5 \cdot x[\text{Widget3}] = s + d \)
    -   Constraint 5 (CatalystX Sales Cap): CatalystX sales cannot exceed 1,500 kg/month:
        -   \( s \leq 1,500 \)
    -   Constraint 6 (Non-negativity): All decision variables must be non-negative:
        -   \( x[i] \geq 0 \) for all widgets \( i \)
        -   \( s \geq 0 \)
        -   \( d \geq 0 \)
